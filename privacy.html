Privacy Policy – Kahani Wala Uploader

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who uses this app

Kahani Wala Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Kahani Wala YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through the YouTube API Services, the app:

  • uploads the channel's own videos and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, schedules and playlists;
  • reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views and watch time) to decide which kinds of stories to make next.

It does not access any viewer's personal data, comments, contacts or anything outside the owner's own channel.

How the data is kept

The sign-in token and the channel's analytics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared with or sent to anyone else, and are used only to run this channel. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The channel's videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube. The app collects no information from children or from any viewer.

Google and YouTube

This app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or through the Google security settings page. The stored token can then be deleted from the owner's computer.
